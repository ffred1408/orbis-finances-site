About Orbis Finances

Orbis Finances is a corporate finance and strategic advisory firm serving executive teams, institutions and corporate groups.

Governance structure

Orbis Finances is intended to be held by Groupe Aequa Mundi (95%), SC Orbis Portage (2.5%) and Frédéric Fougeray (2.5%), subject to final legal structuring.

Strategic vision

The firm develops a disciplined advisory platform dedicated to corporate finance, insurance and financing intermediation across Europe and Africa.
